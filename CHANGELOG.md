# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-07)

The first version.

**Recording**
- Records League matches automatically, with no overlay, injection or reading game memory.
- Uses the Mac's hardware video encoder, so your FPS stays where it was.
- Recordings survive crashes and force quits. Closing the window while you're in a game keeps recording.
- Bookmark a moment mid-game with F8.

**Reviewing**
- Kills, deaths, assists and objectives are marked on a timeline that zooms for long games.
- Notes in Markdown, pinned to the moment they're about.
- Draw on a paused frame and save the drawing with a note.
- Playback speed, frame-by-frame stepping and J/K/L shuttle.
- Hover the timeline to preview any moment.

**Learning**
- Matchups by role: your lane opponent, filtered by any ally or enemy champion.
- Star moments and keep notes per matchup.

**Clips**
- Save any moment or range as a clip, sized to fit Discord if you want.
- Clips are HEVC by default: about three times smaller than before. H.264 is still there for old devices.
- Watch clips in the app; they're copied to your clipboard too.

**Library and storage**
- Split recordings of one game are joined automatically.
- Keep only the moments of a game to save space, one game at a time or many.
- Automatic cleanup by age or size. Games with notes are kept.
- Recently deleted, with Restore and Delete forever.
- Export and import everything, settings included.

**Everywhere**
- ⌘K to search and run commands, ? for every shortcut.
- Keyboard and mouse back/forward, and Finder-style selection.
- A setup page that checks permissions and explains how to fix anything missing.
- Update checks are off until you turn them on. Updates only when you click Update, and only releases signed with the Timewarp release key. If a new version doesn't start, the previous one is put back.
